Privacy

The catalog (at most once a week) and the previews are loaded from Google's servers, with no account or key. A downloaded family is stored in the library, in the “Google Fonts” folder.

Connection

Applications

When a document opens, the panel asks Typeshelf for the missing fonts: the ones in the library are activated, the others can come from Google Fonts. Word, Pages, Keynote, Figma and most apps see active fonts without a plugin.

Illustrator and After Effects only load unsigned extensions in Adobe's debug mode: installing one turns it on, and removing the last one turns it back off.

Security

Plugins talk to Typeshelf on this computer only (127.0.0.1). Web pages cannot use this connection.
